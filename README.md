## 📰 Workday Financial Management — What's New

*Last updated: October 1, 2026*

- 🧾 **2026R2 Puts Agents Inside the Close** — Live in production since Sept. 19, the finance half of the release leads with the Revenue Contract Agent, which drafts customer contracts from uploaded signed documents in a side-by-side viewer and answers contract questions in Workday Chat. A new Account Reconciliation Hub centralizes month-end tasks and reports, moving reconciliation from flagging a discrepancy to explaining it with a recommended action.
- ✂️ **525 Roles Cut in Second Reduction of 2026** — Disclosed after the close Sept. 29: roughly 2.5% of staff, concentrated in Product & Technology, with $65–85M in charges landing mostly in Q3 FY2027 and pushing GAAP operating margin about 21 points below non-GAAP for the quarter. Q3 and full-year guidance were reiterated; shares closed Sept. 30 at $189.17, up 0.3%.
- 📊 **Rising and Analyst Day Now Two Weeks Out** — Rising runs Oct. 12–15 in Las Vegas under an "agentic HR and agentic finance" banner, with 400+ sessions, role-based finance keynotes, and an early look at the roadmap. The Financial Analyst Day slots in Oct. 13 at 2:00 p.m. PT with a live webcast and a replay up for at least 90 days — the first detailed strategy read since the reorg.
- 📉 **Street Holds a Cautious Buy** — Consensus across 42 analysts is Buy with a $208.68 average 12-month target, about 5% above the latest price, but recent moves lean defensive: Jefferies trimmed to $220 on a Hold, and Freedom Broker downgraded to Hold while lifting its target to $200.
- 🌏 **India Payroll Folds Into the Workday Contract** — Announced Sept. 22, Workday Payroll provided by Neeyamo lets customers buy India payroll on a single Workday contract, with certified Global Payroll Connect integration, synchronized HR and payroll data, and localized statutory and compliance updates.

> **Take-Private Talk Still Hangs Over the Reorg** — Workday filed the 525-role cut as a realignment of team structures around its growth strategy rather than a retrenchment, with restructuring work running into fiscal 2028, following February's ~400-role reduction in global customer operations. Unresolved since August is reporting that Silver Lake has been in months-long talks to take the company private at a valuation in the $43–51B range — a deal that would rank among the largest software buyouts ever and would almost certainly need co-investors. No formal terms have surfaced, which makes the Oct. 13 analyst day the most consequential in years.
