## 📰 Workday Financial Management — What's New

*Last updated: November 23, 2026*

- 🚀 **Workday GO for Financials Launches at Rising EMEA** — Unveiled Nov. 18 in Barcelona, Workday GO for Financials is a packaged, transparently priced way for growing companies (sub-3,500 employees) to buy, deploy, and run Financial Management, with industry add-ons for financial services, professional/business services, and tech & media; it's live now in the U.S., U.K., Ireland, and Canada, with more markets to follow.
- 🤖 **Accounting & Deployment Agents Are Generally Available** — The GO package bundles an Accounting Agent (now GA) that follows each customer's existing approval rules to gather audit records and prep reports, plus a GA Deployment Agent that guides setup and recommends next steps for ongoing maintenance; broader "autopilot" capabilities teased when Accounting Agent first launched (Oct. 13) are still slated for early adopters in early 2027.
- 💳 **Zuora Billing and Revenue Now Live Inside Financial Management** — Workday and Zuora's expanded partnership (Nov. 12) gives Financial Management customers direct, in-app access to Zuora Billing and Zuora Revenue via the Zuora Monetization Suite, so usage-based charges and revenue post straight into Workday's Accounting Center and general ledger.
- 📅 **Q3 FY2027 Earnings Land Tomorrow, Nov. 24** — Workday reports fiscal 2027 third-quarter results after market close tomorrow (1:30 p.m. PT call, live webcast on its investor site) — the next read on Financial Management and agent-adoption momentum against full-year guidance of $9.94–9.95B subscription revenue and a 31% non-GAAP operating margin.
- 🐐 **C1.ai Deal Still Pending Close** — Workday's October-announced acquisition of identity-governance platform C1.ai, which will govern what people and AI agents can access across Financial Management and HR, remains on track for close in Workday's fiscal Q4 (ending Jan. 31, 2027); no regulatory or closing update has surfaced yet.

> **Silver Lake Take-Private Talks Still Unresolved** — Reuters' Aug. 13 report of Silver Lake-led talks to take Workday private at a reported $43–51B valuation remains the last word publicly; neither side has commented since, and co-founders Aneel Bhusri and David Duffield's combined 68% voting control means no deal proceeds without them. With Q3 earnings landing tomorrow, Nov. 24, that call is the next moment the subject could resurface.
