## 📰 Workday Financial Management — What's New

*Last updated: October 4, 2026*

- 🧾 **2026R2 Puts Agents Inside the Close** — Live in production since Sept. 19, the finance half of the release leads with the Revenue Contract Agent: its Contract Creation skill drafts customer contracts from uploaded signed documents in a side-by-side viewer with field-level annotations, while Revenue Contract Insights answers questions about the contract in Workday Chat and flags risks before they hit downstream accounting. The new Account Reconciliation Hub — gated by its own `Process: Account Reconciliation` security domain — gives the close a single place to monitor progress and prioritize work, moving reconciliation from flagging a discrepancy to explaining it with a recommended action.
- 💹 **The AI Line Item Is Now Real Money** — The clearest read on whether agentic finance is selling: AI SKU ARR is approaching $600M, up more than 200% year over year and 20% sequentially, with AI products contributing $100M+ in new ACV — over a quarter of all new ACV. More than 5,500 customers now run at least one organic Workday agent, up 35%+ in a single quarter. That is the number to watch for a finance org deciding how hard to lean into Illuminate.
- 📊 **Rising and Analyst Day Now Eight Days Out** — Rising runs Oct. 12–15 in Las Vegas under an "agentic HR and agentic finance" banner, with 400+ sessions, role-based finance keynotes, and an early look at the roadmap. The Financial Analyst Day slots in Oct. 13 at 2:00 p.m. PT with a live webcast and a replay up for at least 90 days — the first detailed strategy read since the reorg.
- 📉 **The Street Walks In Split** — Heading into that analyst day, consensus is conspicuously undecided: roughly 13 buys against 20 holds and 2 sells, with an average target near $214 but estimates spanning $92 to $275 — one of the widest ranges in large-cap software. Shares closed Oct. 2 at $186.14. Management is guiding FY27 subscription revenue to $9.94–9.95B (+13%) and raised non-GAAP operating margin to 31.0%, so the gap is about belief in AI monetization, not the near-term model.
- 💳 **Credit Line Upsized to $1.5B, Maturity Out to 2031** — The 8-K for the revolving credit agreement entered Oct. 1 replaces the April 2022 facility and lifts it from $1B to $1.5B, borrowable through Oct. 1, 2031 with the option to ask lenders twice for a one-year extension. Wells Fargo is administrative agent, swing line lender and L/C issuer; Bank of America, Barclays and Morgan Stanley Senior Funding are syndication agents and joint lead arrangers. Nothing was drawn at signing — dry powder, not a need.

> **Expansion and Retrenchment in the Same Week** — Opening a Dubai hub and standing up UAE operations on Oct. 1, four days after cutting 525 roles (~2.5% of staff, concentrated in Product & Technology, $65–80M in charges running into fiscal 2028), is the clearest signal yet of where spend is being redirected rather than removed. Incoming CMO Sarah Kennedy Ellis, hired from Google, starts Oct. 5 and inherits the job of telling that story to customers and the Street in the same week Rising opens. Still unresolved since August: reporting that Silver Lake has been in months-long talks to take the company private at a $43–51B valuation, with financing work said to be continuing as of mid-September and no formal terms surfaced. It would rank among the largest software buyouts ever and would need co-investors — and with founders Bhusri and Duffield controlling 68% of the vote, nothing moves without them. That silence is what makes the Oct. 13 analyst day the most consequential in years.
