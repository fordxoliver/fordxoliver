## 📰 Workday Financial Management — What's New

*Last updated: August 31, 2026*

- 📊 **Q2 FY2027 Beat Overshadowed by Backlog Miss** — Workday's Aug 27 report showed revenue of $2.65B (+12.8% YoY) and non-GAAP EPS of $2.75 (vs. $2.61 estimate), but subscription revenue backlog grew just 8% to $27.4B versus the Street's ~$28.6B estimate, tempering the headline beat.
- 🤖 **Agentic AI Revenue Nears $600M** — ARR from Workday's agentic AI offerings is approaching $600M, up more than 200% YoY, with AI products driving over 25% of new ACV and 5,500+ customers now running Illuminate agents, including the Financial Close and Audit agents central to Financial Management.
- 💰 **New $4B Buyback Announced** — Alongside earnings, Workday authorized an additional $4 billion in share repurchases, a signal the board views the stock as undervalued even amid ongoing buyout speculation.
- ⚠️ **Guidance Cools the Growth Outlook** — Q3 cRPO growth was guided to 11–12% (below the Street's ~14% expectation) and FY2028 subscription growth is projected at roughly 11%, down from Q2's 14% pace, tempering enthusiasm despite the beat.
- 📉 **Shares Give Back Gains on Macro Headwinds** — WDAY fell about 3.3% on Aug 31 as renewed U.S.-Iran hostilities drove a broader risk-off selloff, cooling some of the post-earnings and buyout-speculation momentum from earlier in the week.

> **Silver Lake Talks Remain Unresolved** — Despite the ~$43B take-private speculation that surfaced August 13, Workday still has not confirmed or denied the reported talks, leaving investors to weigh strong subscription growth and the new buyback against still-unconfirmed buyout interest more than two weeks after the report first broke.
