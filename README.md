## 📰 Workday Financial Management — What's New

*Last updated: October 3, 2026*

- 💳 **Credit Line Upsized to $1.5B, Maturity Out to 2031** — The 8-K for the revolving credit agreement entered Oct. 1 replaces the April 2022 facility and lifts it from $1B to $1.5B, with revolvers borrowable through Oct. 1, 2031 and the option to ask lenders twice for a one-year extension. Wells Fargo is administrative agent, swing line lender and L/C issuer; Bank of America, Barclays and Morgan Stanley Senior Funding are syndication agents and joint lead arrangers. Nothing was drawn at signing.
- 🌍 **UAE Operations Open with a Dubai Hub** — Also on Oct. 1, Workday stood up dedicated regional operations anchored by a new Dubai office and expanded local leadership, with EMEA president Angelique De Vries-Schipperijn and META group VP Zakaria Haltout on hand. The pitch is modernizing HR *and* finance for the UAE's national transformation push, backed by a new report finding 90% of UAE employees now use AI at least weekly. Shares slipped ~3% on the day and closed Oct. 2 at $186.14.
- 🔗 **Payroll and Verification Plumbing Goes Live** — Eligible U.S. workers can route paychecks into Cash App starting Oct. 1 via Workday's Enhanced Direct Deposit interface. Pairing with it: the Experian Verify integration, which automates income and employment verification across a path to 80M+ worker records — more than half of U.S. nonfarm payroll — and reaches customers through the newly launched Workday Total Benefits later this month.
- 🧾 **2026R2 Puts Agents Inside the Close** — Live in production since Sept. 19, the finance half of the release leads with the Revenue Contract Agent, which drafts customer contracts from uploaded signed documents in a side-by-side viewer and answers contract questions in Workday Chat. A new Account Reconciliation Hub centralizes month-end tasks and reports, moving reconciliation from flagging a discrepancy to explaining it with a recommended action.
- 📊 **Rising and Analyst Day Now Ten Days Out** — Rising runs Oct. 12–15 in Las Vegas under an "agentic HR and agentic finance" banner, with 400+ sessions, role-based finance keynotes, and an early look at the roadmap. The Financial Analyst Day slots in Oct. 13 at 2:00 p.m. PT with a live webcast and a replay up for at least 90 days — the first detailed strategy read since the reorg.

> **Expansion and Retrenchment in the Same Week** — Opening Dubai four days after cutting 525 roles (~2.5% of staff, concentrated in Product & Technology, $65–80M in charges running into fiscal 2028) is the clearest signal yet of where spend is being redirected rather than removed. Incoming CMO Sarah Kennedy Ellis, hired from Google, starts Oct. 5 and inherits the job of telling that story to customers and the Street in the same week. Still unresolved since August: reporting that Silver Lake has been in months-long talks to take the company private at a $43–51B valuation, a deal that would rank among the largest software buyouts ever and would need co-investors — with founders Bhusri and Duffield controlling 68% of the vote, nothing moves without them. No formal terms have surfaced, which makes the Oct. 13 analyst day the most consequential in years.
