## 📰 Workday Financial Management — What's New

*Last updated: October 13, 2026*

- 🤖 **Workday Opens Its Agent Platform at Rising** — On Oct. 13 Workday said its Trusted Agent Platform is now headless, letting customers build and run HR and finance agents with their own tools and models. Agent-Ready Tools, Workday Data Cloud, Developer Agent, Custom Agents and the Agent Harness are generally available, with Workday's permissions, approvals and audit logging applied to every agent action. Rising runs through Oct. 15, and the Financial Analyst Day is today at 2:00 p.m. PT (webcast, replay for 90+ days).
- 🧾 **2026R2 Finance Is Live and Worth Auditing Now** — In production since Sept. 19. The finance half leads with the Revenue Contract Agent, which drafts customer contracts from uploaded signed documents in a side-by-side viewer and answers contract questions in Workday Chat. Alongside it: an Account Reconciliation Hub that centralizes month-end tasks and progress tracking, high-volume grids on reconciliations, automated Procure-to-Pay exception handling, and new Contract-to-Cash and Operational-to-Accounting capability. The theme is moving reconciliation from *flagging* a discrepancy to *explaining* it with a recommended action.
- 💳 **Credit Line Upsized to $1.5B, Maturity Out to 2031** — The Oct. 1 revolving credit agreement replaces the April 2022 facility and lifts it from $1B to $1.5B, borrowable through Oct. 1, 2031 with the option to ask lenders twice for a one-year extension. Wells Fargo is administrative agent, swing line lender and L/C issuer; Bank of America, Barclays and Morgan Stanley Senior Funding are syndication agents and joint lead arrangers. Nothing was drawn at signing — dry powder sitting in plain sight ahead of a contested autumn.
- 🧠 **Autopilot Agents and Decision Intelligence Land Across Finance** — Workday also announced Autopilot Agents for HR, finance and IT, and Decision Intelligence to weigh workforce and financial decisions together. Partners are piling in: Auditoria.AI's agentic Cash Cycle (AP and AR) and Trintech's governed reconciliation, accruals and exception agents are both showing at Rising. Critics note the governance layer is the most mature part; finance agents still cluster in close, controls, contracts and planning.
- 📉 **The Street Can't Agree on What This Is Worth** — Shares closed at $186.14 on Oct. 2, but consensus targets are scattered from a $92 low to $326 high depending on who's polled, with several firms cutting sharply on slower sales trends, revised long-term guidance and heavier AI spend weighing on margins. A "Buy" consensus that spans a 3.5x range isn't a view — it's a coin flip waiting for Oct. 13. CFO Zane Rowe also sold ~6,000 shares (~$1.12M) on Oct. 6, and one report (single-source, unconfirmed) says the 2028 revenue growth target was cut, with Jefferies expecting growth to slow to ~10% by fiscal 2028.

> **A New CMO Takes the Helm Into the Loudest Possible Week** — Sarah Kennedy Ellis takes over global marketing and communications effective Oct. 5, reporting directly to Aneel Bhusri, after leading marketing for Google Cloud and, before that, Adobe Experience Cloud and Marketo. She inherits a narrative with three live wires: 525 roles cut in late September (~2.5% of staff, concentrated in Product & Technology, $65–80M in charges running into fiscal 2028), a brand-new Dubai hub opened four days later, and — still unresolved since August — reporting that Silver Lake has been in months-long talks to take the company private at a $43–51B valuation, among the largest software buyouts ever attempted. With founders Bhusri and Duffield controlling 68% of the vote, nothing moves without them, and no formal terms have surfaced. The analyst day happening today has to reconcile all of it.
