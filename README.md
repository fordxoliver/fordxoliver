The Financial Analyst Day slots in Oct. 13 at 2:00 p.m. PT with a live webcast and a replay up for at least 90 days — the first detailed strategy read since the reorg, and the week everything below gets re-priced.
- 🧾 **2026R2 Finance Is Live and Worth Auditing Now** — In production since Sept. 19. The finance half leads with the Revenue Contract Agent, which drafts customer contracts from uploaded signed documents in a side-by-side viewer and answers contract questions in Workday Chat. Alongside it: an Account Reconciliation Hub that centralizes month-end tasks and progress tracking, high-volume grids on reconciliations, automated Procure-to-Pay exception handling, and new Contract-to-Cash and Operational-to-Accounting capability. The theme is moving reconciliation from *flagging* a discrepancy to *explaining* it with a recommended action.

Wells Fargo is administrative agent, swing line lender and L/C issuer; Bank of America, Barclays and Morgan Stanley Senior Funding are syndication agents and joint lead arrangers. Nothing was drawn at signing — dry powder sitting in plain sight ahead of a contested autumn.

New Workday Financial Management customers get a two-month free SmartResearch test drive and limited-time annual pricing through year-end, with a showing at Rising Oct. 12–15 — more proof the partner ecosystem is racing to fill the agentic finance gap.
