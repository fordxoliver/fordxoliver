## 📰 Workday Financial Management — What's New

*Last updated: October 2, 2026*

- 💳 **Credit Line Upsized to $1.5B, Maturity Out to 2031** — An 8-K filed this week covers a new revolving credit agreement entered Oct. 1 that replaces the April 2022 facility and lifts it from $1B to $1.5B, with revolvers borrowable through Oct. 1, 2031 and the option to ask lenders twice for a one-year extension. Wells Fargo is administrative agent, swing line lender and L/C issuer; Bank of America, Barclays and Morgan Stanley Senior Funding are syndication agents and joint lead arrangers. Nothing was drawn at signing.
- 🧾 **2026R2 Puts Agents Inside the Close** — Live in production since Sept. 19, the finance half of the release leads with the Revenue Contract Agent, which drafts customer contracts from uploaded signed documents in a side-by-side viewer and answers contract questions in Workday Chat. A new Account Reconciliation Hub centralizes month-end tasks and reports, moving reconciliation from flagging a discrepancy to explaining it with a recommended action.
- ✂️ **525 Roles Cut in Second Reduction of 2026** — Disclosed after the close Sept. 29: roughly 2.5% of staff, concentrated in Product & Technology, plus select office-lease reductions. The 8-K puts charges at $65–80M — $55–70M in Q3 FY2027, $10M in Q4 — of which $40–55M is cash severance and benefits and ~$15M a non-cash lease impairment, with employee actions substantially complete by Q1 FY2028. Q3 and full-year guidance were reiterated; shares closed Sept. 30 at $189.17, up 0.3%.
- 📊 **Rising and Analyst Day Now Ten Days Out** — Rising runs Oct. 12–15 in Las Vegas under an "agentic HR and agentic finance" banner, with 400+ sessions, role-based finance keynotes, and an early look at the roadmap. The Financial Analyst Day slots in Oct. 13 at 2:00 p.m. PT with a live webcast and a replay up for at least 90 days — the first detailed strategy read since the reorg.
- 🧭 **New CMO Starts Oct. 5** — Sarah Kennedy Ellis, hired from Google, takes over as chief marketing officer days before Rising, inheriting the job of selling the agentic-finance story to customers and the Street in the same week.

> **Take-Private Talk Still Hangs Over the Reorg** — Workday filed the 525-role cut as a realignment of team structures around its growth strategy rather than a retrenchment, with restructuring work running into fiscal 2028, following February's ~400-role reduction in global customer operations. Unresolved since August is reporting that Silver Lake has been in months-long talks to take the company private at a valuation in the $43–51B range — a deal that would rank among the largest software buyouts ever and would almost certainly need co-investors. No formal terms have surfaced, and an upsized, undrawn $1.5B revolver read against that backdrop is the kind of balance-sheet housekeeping that cuts either way — which makes the Oct. 13 analyst day the most consequential in years.
